Governance and Oversight of the UC–CSU–CCC AI Computing Program

UC San Diego IT Services · October 2026 · Draft for discussion

Recommendation: Build oversight on bodies that already exist, and add only two small program committees: a steering committee and a faculty allocation committee.

  • The UC Office of the President owns the system, as the gift agreement requires.
  • The three public systems (UC, CSU and the California Community Colleges) oversee it together through the California Education Round Table, which already brings their leaders together.
  • A small steering committee handles governance day to day.
  • UC San Diego operates the system.

Proposed structure

What the committee oversees

The planned system at SDSC has 15 GPU racks, divided by use:

Part of the system Size Who uses it, and how they get access
High-performance research 8 racks Research groups running large training and simulation jobs; time is awarded by the allocation committee
General research 4 racks, one of them a secure enclave for regulated data Faculty and graduate researchers; enclave users are vetted for regulated-data rules
Teaching and AI models 3 racks, shifted between the two across the academic year Instructors request course environments each term; AI model use is tracked by project and campus

The steering committee can change this split each year as demand becomes clear.

Who does what

Body Role
California Education Round Table (UC President, CSU and CCC Chancellors) Joint oversight: endorses access principles for the three systems and receives an annual report
UC Office of the President Owner: approves the budget, equipment purchases and refresh
Program steering committee (chaired by UCOP; members from UC San Diego, CSU, CCC and faculty) Day-to-day governance: sets each system's share, approves the annual plan, oversees operations
UC San Diego (SDSC and IT Services) Host and operator: SDSC runs the systems, data center and cooling, and supports research users; IT Services runs the teaching platform, accounts and user support
Allocation committee (faculty from all three systems) Awards research time on the high-performance and general research racks

How it would work

  • Fair access. Each year the steering committee sets the split between research and teaching, and a guaranteed share for each system, so no system crowds out the others.
  • One contact per system. UC campuses and the CSU and community college system offices each name an access lead. Individual users sign in and request access themselves.
  • No charge to users. Students and faculty use the system free. As the gift agreement anticipates, operations and equipment refresh are paid for by institutional contributions, research grants where allowed, state support and philanthropy.
  • Accountability. An annual report to the Round Table and the donor covers use by system, results and finances.
